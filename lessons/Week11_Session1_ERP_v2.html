Definition

ERP integrates core business processes

Enterprise Resource Planning connects shared data and workflows across finance, sales, purchasing, inventory, human resources, production, and reporting.

One transaction can affect multiple modules through controlled rules.

Accounting connection

Finance is the integration test

Sales, purchasing, payroll, inventory, and assets all eventually affect financial records. ERP gives finance visibility, but only if master data and posting rules are reliable.

Master data

Shared data must have ownership

  • Customer master.
  • Supplier master.

  • Employee master.
02

Benefits
and risks

Benefits

Why organizations adopt ERP

  • Single source of truth.
  • Fewer duplicate entries.
  • Faster reporting.
  • Cross-functional visibility.
  • Standardized controls.
Risk

Integration magnifies both value and error

A wrong supplier bank account, weak role, or bad master record can affect many transactions. ERP needs governance, testing, access control, and change discipline.

ERP controls worth checking

  • Role permissions.
  • Approval workflow.
  • Master-data change controls.
  • Three-way matching.
  • Exception reporting.
  • Audit trail and period close.

Quiz 1–5

1. ERP integrates: a) core business processes b) only email c) only payroll d) only reports

2. Shared master data includes: a) suppliers b) lunch c) passwords only d) rumors

3. ERP value comes from: a) integration b) isolation c) duplicate entry d) no controls

4. Finance is affected by: a) sales and purchasing b) nothing else c) only tax d) only HR

5. Three-way match compares: a) PO, receipt, invoice b) password, user, role c) sales, tax, HR d) none

Quiz 6–10

6. Master-data change should be: a) controlled b) open to all c) hidden d) unlogged

7. ERP can reduce: a) duplicate entry b) accountability c) visibility d) controls

8. A wrong supplier record can affect: a) many transactions b) nothing c) only a logo d) only training

9. ERP implementation requires: a) testing and change management b) no training c) no access review d) no migration plan

10. ERP audit trails support: a) traceability b) secrecy c) no evidence d) random access

Answer key

Answers & rationales

1-A: ERP is enterprise integration. 2-A: supplier master is shared data. 3-A: integration creates value. 4-A: many processes affect finance. 5-A: three-way match. 6-A: master changes need controls. 7-A: integration reduces duplicate entry. 8-A: shared records have broad impact. 9-A: testing/training are essential. 10-A: logs preserve traceability.
